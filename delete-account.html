Bol Khata - Account & Data Deletion

If you want to delete your Bol Khata account and all associated data, you can follow these simple steps:

  1. Send an email to our support team at your-email@gmail.com with the subject line "Request for Account Deletion".
  2. Include your registered email address or phone number in the message body.
  3. Our team will verify your request and permanently delete your account, ledger entries, and personal records within 7 business days.

Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
